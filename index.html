Core OS & Architectural Concepts
Priority Scheduling: Dispatches processes strictly according to urgency (Priority 1 = Highest). Low-priority processes risk starvation under continuous arrivals.
Multilevel Queue (MLQ): Partitions the ready queue into distinct tiers (System, Interactive, Background) matching process characteristics and scheduling algorithms.
Preemptive Hierarchy: System tasks (Q1) take absolute precedence over Interactive (Q2) and Background (Q3) tasks, preempting lower-tier execution slices.
Stage 3 Roadmap (MQAPS): To prevent Background (Q3) starvation observed in traditional MLQ, Multi-Queue Adaptive Priority Scheduling introduces dynamic aging and migration.
